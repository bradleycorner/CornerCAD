## Socket types across the lamp line

Every lamp ships as printed parts and needs a kit the customer must source. Worth stocking the
E26 and E14 kits the way the 5V USB one already is — otherwise each $60 lamp sends the buyer
elsewhere for the $10 part that makes it work, and they may buy the wrong one.

## Materials note

Drift mixes PLA (body) with PETG (connector and shade). PETG next to a bulb is a *functional*
choice — it tolerates heat far better than PLA — so it belongs in the copy as a selling point,
not just a spec. Anyone printing the same model entirely in PLA has a worse product.

⚠️ Cost: a mixed-material print purges on every material change. On the Wall Clock that was
16.22 g of 66.54 g — **26% waste**. Check the *flushed* figure in the slicer before pricing, and
batch plates to amortise it.

## Drift Lamp — CAD-LMP-0001

### Summary

A modern minimalist table lamp that twist-locks together from modular printed parts, with a
spiral-wound PETG shade that glows evenly when lit. Roughly 220 mm across and 275 mm tall
assembled. Supplied as printed parts — requires a standard E26 lamp kit, sold separately.

### Details

The **Drift Lamp** is built to go together in about a minute and come apart just as easily. The
power cable threads up through the body, the socket fixture seats into a connector, and the
connector twist-locks into the lamp body. The shade drops over the top and rests in its own lip —
no screws, no glue, nothing visible holding it together.

Every lamp is 3D printed to order:

- **Printed in Two Materials:** The body is PLA; the connector and shade are **PETG**, which tolerates heat far better than PLA — the right choice for parts sitting close to a bulb.
- **Spiral-Wound Shade:** The shade is printed as one continuous wall, so there are no seams and light passes through evenly rather than banding at layer joins.
- **Two Connector Sizes:** Fits socket fixtures with either a 30 mm or 40 mm inner diameter, so it works with the common E26 kits rather than one specific brand.
- **Modular Assembly:** Threads, seats, twist-locks and drops together. Disassembles just as easily for cleaning or a change of color.
- **Substantial Scale:** Shade 219 mm across and 120 mm tall over a 161 mm body — a table lamp, not an accent piece.
- **Color Customization:** Any color from our filament range, with the shade and body independently colored.

**Requires a standard E26 lamp kit with plug and switch, sold separately.** Supplied as printed
parts only: no electrical components, cable, socket, or bulb included. **Use low-heat LED bulbs
only, 25 W maximum** — do not fit incandescent or halogen bulbs.

- ⚠️CONFIRM **Connectors supplied:** The design includes both a 30 mm and a 40 mm connector. State whether the customer receives both, or picks one when ordering.
