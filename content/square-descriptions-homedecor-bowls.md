## ⚠️ One question for Bradley

The `⚠️CONFIRM` bullet on the **Horizon Cake Plate** is about **direct food contact**. A cake
plate implies cake sitting on the surface, and FDM prints are generally not recommended for
direct food contact — layer lines trap residue and are hard to sanitize. The Halloween and Xmas
bowls are lower risk because they're photographed with wrapped candy.

Three options: state it's for wrapped or covered items only, state that it's sealed/food-safe
if you finish it that way, or drop the line entirely. Your call — I won't guess about a food
claim on a live store. The push script blocks while the tag remains.

## Gaia Trays — CAD-BWL-0001

### Summary

A coordinated catch-all set — a soft stadium-shaped tray with a contrasting inset liner, a
matching cylindrical cup, and a domed caddy with a perforated top. Built to sit together on an
entry table or a desk. 3D printed to order in your choice of colors.

### Details

**Gaia** is a set rather than a single object. The pieces share one rounded, tactile language —
softened corners, a fine matte surface, and a recessed inner panel picked out in a contrasting

Every piece is 3D printed to order:

## Haven Bowl — CAD-BWL-0004

### Summary

The **Haven Bowl** is about weight and shadow. Undulating ribs run the full height of the outer
wall and pinch as they meet the base, so the surface is never flat and never quite repeats.
Raising it on a pedestal foot lifts the whole shape off the table and lets the shadow work.

Every piece is 3D printed to order:

- **Wave-Carved Wall:** Vertical waves flow around the entire bowl, reading as deep relief in raking light and as quiet texture in soft light.
- **Pedestal Foot:** A tapered base lifts the bowl clear of the surface, giving it presence beyond its footprint.
- **Solid Form:** A closed wall throughout — this one holds what you put in it.
- **Color Customization:** Any color from our filament range. Dark tones emphasize the shadow; pale tones emphasize the form.

Crafted with precision, sustainable materials, and attention to detail.
