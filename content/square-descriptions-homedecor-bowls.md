## Gaia Trays — CAD-BWL-0001

### Summary

A coordinated catch-all set — a soft stadium-shaped tray with a contrasting inset liner, a
matching cylindrical cup, and a domed caddy with a perforated top. Built to sit together on an
entry table or a desk. 3D printed to order in your choice of colors.

### Details

**Gaia** is a set rather than a single object. The pieces share one rounded, tactile language —
softened corners, a fine matte surface, and a recessed inner panel picked out in a contrasting
tone — so they read as a family whether you use one or all of them.

Every piece is 3D printed to order:

- **Inset Contrast Liner:** The tray floor drops into a recessed panel finished in a second color, giving depth to what would otherwise be a flat surface.
- **Matching Cup:** A straight-sided cylinder for pens, brushes, or keys, sized to sit alongside the tray rather than dominate it.
- **Domed Caddy:** A sphere with a perforated top, holding small upright items in individual openings.
- **Color Customization:** Any color from our filament range, with the liner in a second color.

Crafted with precision, sustainable materials, and attention to detail.

## Grideo Catch All Bowl — CAD-BWL-0002

### Summary

A round catch-all with a fine micro-grid texture wrapping the outside and a smooth interior
split into two compartments by a single sweeping curve. Keys on one side, everything else on
the other. 3D printed to order in your choice of colors.

### Details

The **Grideo Catch All Bowl** does two things at once: a densely knurled exterior that reads as
texture from across the room, and a clean, uninterrupted interior that's easy to sweep out.

Every piece is 3D printed to order:

- **Micro-Grid Exterior:** A fine repeating grid covers the outer wall, catching light at every angle and giving the bowl real grip.
- **Divided Interior:** One S-curve splits the inside into two unequal bays — keys and cards in one, coins and small items in the other.
- **Two-Tone Construction:** The textured shell and the smooth interior print in separate colors, so the division reads at a glance.
- **Color Customization:** Any color from our filament range.

Crafted with precision, sustainable materials, and attention to detail.

## Haven Bowl — CAD-BWL-0004

### Summary

A footed bowl raised on a pedestal, its outer wall carved into a rhythm of soft vertical waves.
Substantial enough to anchor a coffee table on its own. 3D printed to order in your choice of
color.

### Details

The **Haven Bowl** is about weight and shadow. Undulating ribs run the full height of the outer
wall and pinch as they meet the base, so the surface is never flat and never quite repeats.
Raising it on a pedestal foot lifts the whole shape off the table and lets the shadow work.

Every piece is 3D printed to order:

- **Wave-Carved Wall:** Vertical waves flow around the entire bowl, reading as deep relief in raking light and as quiet texture in soft light.
- **Pedestal Foot:** A tapered base lifts the bowl clear of the surface, giving it presence beyond its footprint.
- **Solid Form:** A closed wall throughout — this one holds what you put in it.
- **Color Customization:** Any color from our filament range. Dark tones emphasize the shadow; pale tones emphasize the form.

Crafted with precision, sustainable materials, and attention to detail.

Designed by h3li0.

## Helix Bowls — CAD-BWL-0005

### Summary

A low, wide bowl banded with an open helix lattice — twisting ribs cut clean through the wall,
framed by a solid rim above and a solid base below. 3D printed to order in your choice of color.

### Details

The **Helix Bowl** puts all its detail in one band. Ribs spiral around the wall and cross each
other as they climb, leaving open diamonds between them, while solid bands top and bottom keep
the silhouette crisp and the structure rigid.

Every piece is 3D printed to order:

- **Open Helix Band:** The twisting lattice is cut through the wall, casting a moving shadow pattern onto whatever sits beneath it.
- **Framed Silhouette:** Solid rim and base contain the openwork, so the bowl reads as deliberate rather than busy.
- **For Dry Display:** The pierced wall means this is a fruit-and-objects bowl, not a liquid one.
- **Color Customization:** Any color from our filament range.

Crafted with precision, sustainable materials, and attention to detail.

## Horizon Cake Plate — CAD-BWL-0006

### Summary

A wide serving plate ringed by a pierced wave lattice, resting on a stand that twist-locks
underneath — and hides a compartment inside. Twist to lift the plate away and there's discreet
storage waiting, sized for candles or anything small you'd rather not leave out. 3D printed to
order in your choice of colors.

### Details

The **Horizon Cake Plate** looks like a clean disc from above and reveals itself from every
other angle. The full rim is an unbroken band of open, interlocking waves cut clean through the
edge. Underneath, the tapered stand isn't solid — the plate twist-locks onto it, and lifting it
free opens a hidden compartment.

Every piece is 3D printed to order:

- **Twist-Lock Hidden Storage:** The plate detaches with a simple twist to reveal a concealed compartment in the stand — small candles, matches, or keepsakes, out of sight until you want them.
- **Pierced Rim Band:** A continuous wave lattice runs the full circumference, cut through so the edge catches light from beneath.
- **Smooth or Textured Stand:** The stand can be printed with a fine textured finish or left smooth — your choice when you order.
- **Generous Size:** The plate is roughly 249 mm across (about 9.8"), standing 94 mm tall on its base — a real cake plate, not a coaster.
- **Two-Tone Construction:** Plate and stand print separately and are independently colored.
- **Please Use Parchment:** We recommend parchment paper rather than placing cake directly on the plate. This is the designer's own guidance and we pass it on.

Crafted with precision, sustainable materials, and attention to detail.

Designed by h3li0.
