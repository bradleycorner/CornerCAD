### What I take on

Real, functional custom work — not just "put my logo on it." A few examples of what fits:

- A bracket, mount, or adapter that doesn't exist off the shelf.
- A replacement part for something discontinued, reverse-engineered to fit.
- Engraved signage or a personalized coaster/keepsake in wood or slate.
- A one-off vase, planter, or lamp sized to a specific spot.
- A prototype you need turned from an idea into a physical part.

If it needs to actually *fit* and *work*, that's the point — everything starts as a dimensioned FreeCAD model,
not a guess.

---

### How it works
*(Simple numbered process — keeps expectations clear.)*

1. **Tell me what you need.** Rough sketch, photo, measurements, or just a description — send what you've got.
2. **I scope it.** I'll confirm whether it's a fit, rough timeline, and a price before any work starts.
3. **Design & approve.** I model it in FreeCAD and share it for sign-off before printing.
4. **Made to order.** Once approved, I build it on the right machine for the job and ship it.
