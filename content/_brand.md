# CornerCAD — Brand & Positioning (source of truth)

> Read this first before writing any page. Every page's voice and audience derives from here.

## One-line positioning
Objects you keep and use — designed in FreeCAD, built on real hardware. Not printed novelties.

## Approved taglines
- **#1 (hero / emotional):** Made to be used, not unboxed and forgotten.
- **#2 (support / footer / the "how"):** Designed in FreeCAD. Built to keep.
- **#5 (brand signature / bios):** Parametric design, made physical.

## Voice
Maker-credible, plain-spoken, a little contrarian toward the throwaway-print market. Confident, not
gimmicky. Leads with function and durability. Proof over hype (e.g. "tested on my own Daytona Coupe").

## Two-domain map
| Domain | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **CornerCAD.com** | Direct-to-consumer | The brand hub + retail catalog: lifestyle products (vases, planters, lamps, coasters in wood/slate) + automotive parts. Per-product buy/download/inquire CTA. Light "custom work" on-ramp for individuals. |
| **CornerCADWorks.com** | B2B / wholesale | The manufacturing arm: custom parametric design, wholesale runs, candle molds + beeswax containers, dealer/retail program (ACE Hardware pitch), volume production. Its own landing + inquiry funnel. |

**Cross-linking:** consumer site's Custom Work teaser sends *business/wholesale* inquiries to
CornerCADWorks.com; keeps *individual* one-off custom requests on CornerCAD.com/custom-work/.

## Equipment (proof of capability — use where credibility matters)
- **Creality K2 Plus** — multi-material FDM (functional parts, planters, lamps, automotive)
- **Elegoo Saturn 16K Ultra** — high-resolution resin (fine detail, molds, small parts)
- **Longer Ray5** — CO₂/diode laser (wood & slate engraving, coasters, signage)
