## What we do (capability strip)

- **Custom design.** Turn a sketch, sample, or idea into a dimensioned, production-ready FreeCAD model.
- **Wholesale production.** Recurring runs of a proven product, priced for resale.
- **Molds & tooling.** Master patterns and molds designed to your specs *(our original designs only)*.
- **Multi-process.** Multi-material FDM, high-resolution resin, and laser engraving — matched to the job.

## Programs

### For makers & small manufacturers
Need a **mold** for a repeatable product, or **containers** you can produce in your colors and materials?
We design the tooling and produce to order. *(Original example: wax/candle molds and beeswax candle
containers for honey & candle makers — designed from scratch to your dimensions.)*

- Mold-only, or finished containers — your call.
- Iterate on a sample before committing to a run.
- Consistent, repeatable output.

### For retailers & dealers
Locally made, shelf-ready product for stores that want something that isn't mass-market import. Wholesale
pricing, restock lead times you can plan around, and made right here in Jacksonville, FL.

- Wholesale / dealer pricing on qualifying volume.
- Curated product that fits a local / maker-goods shelf.
- Reliable reorders — we scale FDM production through a print-on-demand farm when volume calls for it.

### Custom & prototyping
One-off parts, replacement components, fixtures, and prototypes — designed to fit and function, delivered
made-to-order.

## How it works

1. **Tell us the goal.** Product, target price, rough volume, timeline.
2. **We scope & quote.** Feasibility, tooling/design cost, unit pricing, lead time — in writing.
3. **Sample first.** Approve a physical sample before any production run.
4. **Produce & reorder.** We build to order and keep reorders predictable.
