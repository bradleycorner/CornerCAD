**Description:**
Cut from natural slate and laser-engraved with a set of four coordinated geometric patterns — no two faces the same, all built to sit together. The stone shrugs off heat, condensation, and the occasional dropped glass; the engraving is burned in, not printed on, so it won't peel or fade.

Cork-backed so they grip the table and never scratch it. Heavy enough to feel like an object you own, not a giveaway you toss. Made to order and finished by hand here in Jacksonville — each set is produced when you order it, so the slate and engraving are fresh, not pulled off a shelf.
